04Aurora outlook

    Three-hour planetary Kp from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center: muted bars are observed, bright bars are forecast. The dashed line is the lowest threshold among your places. The rule of thumb is Kp ≈ (66° − geomagnetic latitude) ÷ 2, using a centred-dipole model. It isn't a forecast. You'll still need dark, clear skies and a view toward the pole.
